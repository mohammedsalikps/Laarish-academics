# Curriculum sources: verified textbook list, Classes 1–8

Research date: **2026-10-21**. Current academic year: **2026-27**.
Scope: NCERT books under NCF-SE 2023 for Maths, English, Hindi, The World Around Us, Science and Social Science. Also the CBSE curriculum pages, the NCF-SE 2023 stage structure, and Kannada (Karnataka state books plus NCERT's new R3 book).

**Status labels**

- **verified (official page)**: read directly from an official site or official PDF (ncert.nic.in, cbseacademic.nic.in, textbooks.karnataka.gov.in, dsert.karnataka.gov.in).
- **verified (official page, image)**: as above, but the PDF had no extractable text, so I read the titles from a rendered page image. The source is official, but there is a small risk of transcription errors (Hindi and Kannada lists).
- **corroborated (secondary source)**: news or other non-official source, used only to support an official finding.
- **not verified**

**How the NCERT data was checked**

1. The book list for each class and subject comes from the dropdown data on `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php`. Books that NCERT has commented out of the dropdown were ignored.
2. Chapter titles and first-edition dates come from each book's official prelims PDF: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/<code>ps.pdf`. This file holds the title page, the imprint and the Contents page.
3. Every prelims PDF used here has a **"Reprint 2026-27"** watermark, except three new first editions (7 Maths Part II, 8 Maths Part II and 8 SS Part 2) and 6 SS (whose imprint shows a February 2026 reprint). So these are the books in use for 2026-27.
4. Each chapter count matches the `0-N` chapter range on the NCERT page.

- **Acad-29/2024** (22 Mar 2024): schools should follow NCERT's new syllabi and textbooks for **Classes 3 and 6** in 2024-25, with a bridge course for Class 6. No change for other classes that year. https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2024/29_Circular_2024.pdf

- **Skill-81/2025** (Oct 2025): Kaushal Bodh textbooks are mandatory in Classes VI–VIII from 2025-26. I read the title only, not the circular body.
- **Acad-15/2026** (1 Apr 2026): CBSE's Computational Thinking and AI curriculum framework for **Classes III–VIII** from 2026-27. https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/15_Circular_2026.pdf

| Stage | Grades / ages | What it covers |
